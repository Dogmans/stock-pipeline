# Candidate discovery and research workflow

The pipeline organizes evidence for research; screening scores do not establish investment attractiveness or forecast returns.

## Broad discovery

Use the [TAM workflow](../workflows/proven_technology_emerging_tam.json) with `fmp_listed` or an explicitly chosen custom universe. This stage applies operating-scale, margin, growth, liquidity and survivability checks. Do not require theme keywords, a Technology sector label or a predefined company list when the objective is discovering unfamiliar operating companies.

Review the exported candidate list and approximately 100 near misses separately. Inspect unknown fields, exact failed rules, source dates, currency conversions and score penalties. Keep all surplus survivors in the full audit instead of tightening thresholds merely to hit an output count.

## Qualitative research

Use the retained metrics and raw financial statements as inputs for human or AI-assisted research: verify business operations, technology maturity, customer adoption, competitive positioning, addressable-market evidence and financial obligations. The repository does not automatically run a qualitative AI TAM-research stage.

Check large dilution changes for corporate actions, EV assumptions for preferred equity/minority interests, and cash runway for restrictions and debt maturities. Ambiguous shell/exploration classifications require review. Benchmark symbols are diagnostics, not exemptions or expected winners.

## Comparing and monitoring

Other registered screens can form independent or sequential research paths. The editor supports ranked sample comparisons, annual financial history and saved-run changes. Forward Performance is limited to outputs of at most 100 stocks; it is subsequent price tracking rather than a backtest. Reduce a broad research universe to a focused monitoring set before using that feature.
